# Weara Privacy Policy

Weara is developed by Ecrin Labs.

Weara can access photos selected by the user and, when enabled, the device camera.
Selected images are used for visual fashion discovery. The application does not
require a paid third-party generative AI API.

Weara does not sell personal information. External shopping links may open third
party websites, whose privacy policies apply independently.
